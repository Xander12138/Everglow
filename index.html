Frequently asked

Where is my journal stored?

On your device. Entries, chats, and everything Everglow learns about your world stay in local storage by default.

Does my writing get sent to the cloud?

Not unless you choose it. The built-in AI runs entirely on-device. You can optionally connect a third-party AI provider with your own API key, or turn on encrypted iCloud backup — both are off by default and clearly opt-in.

How do I back up or move to a new device?

Enable iCloud backup in Settings. Everglow bundles your data into a single encrypted snapshot in your private iCloud, which you can restore during setup on a new device.

How do I lock the app?

Settings → App Lock lets you set a PIN or password and enable Face ID / Touch ID.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes all on-device data. If you enabled iCloud backup, you can remove the snapshot from your iCloud storage settings.
