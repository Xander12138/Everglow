App unlock and hosted AI

Unlimited or Cloud?

Choose a permanent app unlock, an optional hosted-AI subscription, or both. The choice depends on how you want to write and use AI.

Start with the free limits

Try up to 7 journal entries and 10 companion chat turns before choosing how to continue.

Everglow Unlimited

A one-time purchase permanently removes entry and companion-chat limits. Hosted AI is not included.

You can use the on-device model where supported, or bring your own API key. Your provider’s usage charges are separate from the app unlock. A local model needs a download and device support.

Everglow Cloud

An optional subscription supplies hosted AI with a monthly usage allowance. You do not need to prepare an API key or download a local model. Entry and companion-chat limits are removed while the subscription is active.

Cloud request context goes through Everglow’s hosted service to OpenAI. Read how processing works.

Can I choose both?

Yes. Unlimited gives you the permanent app unlock; Cloud provides hosted AI while active. Cloud is optional for an Unlimited customer.

How do trials affect the limits?

An eligible StoreKit introductory subscription trial grants subscription access while its entitlement is active. The device-based Cloud preview keeps the free entry and companion-chat limits. The current offer and its terms are shown in the app.

What does it cost?

Prices, hosted-AI allowances and eligible offers are shown in the app and can vary by storefront. Review the purchase sheet before buying. Unlimited means unlimited entries and companion-chat turns, not unlimited hosted-AI usage.

Purchases are processed by Apple. See the terms for subscription, cancellation and refund details.
