On your device

On-device AI processes writing locally after a model download. Availability and performance depend on your device.

With your own API key

The journal and chat context needed for a request goes to your chosen provider, under its policies and terms. Provider usage charges are separate.

With Everglow Cloud

Optional hosted AI sends request context through Everglow’s service to OpenAI. You don’t need to prepare an API key or download a local model.

You can also write without AI. Optional iCloud backup stores a snapshot in your private CloudKit database.

Before your
first entry.

Can I use Everglow without a subscription?

Yes. You can start with the free limits. Unlimited is a one-time purchase; Everglow Cloud is an optional subscription. You can write without AI, and local or API-key AI does not require a Cloud subscription.

Can I bring my existing journal?

Everglow offers an import review for formats including Markdown, plain text, ZIP, CSV and JSON. Start with a small sample and check dates and text. A complete transfer of media, tags, titles or PDFs from another app is not assured. Read the limitations.

What does Markdown export include?

Dated entries and conversations. Derived summaries, people and facts are rebuilt when imported. Voice recordings are omitted, and the export is unencrypted: store it somewhere private.

Which languages are supported?

The iPhone app supports English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, French and Spanish.
